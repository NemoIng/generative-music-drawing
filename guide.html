Step-by-Step

🚀 30 Seconds to Your First Song

1

Pick a Sound Ink (Color)

At the top of the screen, click on one of the colorful ink circles. Try Indigo (🎻 Bowed Cello) or Amber (🪵 Acoustic Kalimba).

2

Draw a Line Across the Canvas

Click and drag your mouse from left to right. Draw a gentle wave, a straight line, or a mountain curve. As soon as you release, it turns into a glowing musical track!

3

Press Spacebar (Play / Pause)

Watch the little glowing orb (the playhead) ride along your line like a train on a track. Hear it sing notes continuously!

4

Draw a Second Line Above It

Switch to Violet (🔔 Shimmer Bells), select the Dotted (• • •) texture, and draw another curve near the top. Both lines now play together in harmony!

The Fundamentals

🎨 How Drawing Makes Sound

↕️ Up & Down = Pitch (High vs Low)

Think of the canvas like a harp stood upright:

  • Bottom of screen: Deep, warm bass notes (like a cello or sub-bass).
  • Middle of screen: Cozy melodies, guitars, and kalimba tines.
  • Top of screen: Sparkling high bells, crystal chimes, and flutes.

↔️ Left & Right = Time & Stereo Panning

Left-to-right controls both when the note plays and where you hear it in your ears:

  • Stereo Placement: Lines on the left edge sound in your left headphone; lines on the right sound in your right headphone!
  • Looping: The playhead travels from start to finish, looping seamlessly.
🛡️ Why You Can Never Play a "Wrong Note" (The Safety Net)

In traditional instruments like pianos, pressing the wrong key sounds harsh. In Harmonic Geometry, the canvas is automatically locked to ancient harmonious scales (like Dorian, Lydian, and Pentatonic). Every single millimeter you draw snaps to harmonious musical degrees. Whatever you draw will sound serene and in-tune!

🌌 The Scrollable 8-Octave Universe

The screen isn't just one page — it's an 8-octave infinite sky! Scroll your mouse wheel (or drag the mini-map on the right edge) to travel from sub-bass vibrations at the bottom all the way up to celestial frequencies at the top.

Stroke Types

🖌️ The 3 Line Textures

―

Solid Drone

Continuous singing tone (like a bowed cello, synthesizer pad, or glass organ).

✨ Smooth Legato Transition: When your curve slopes from note to note, both notes sound simultaneously in a lush, warm chord before crossfading!
• • •

Dotted Chime

Plucked rhythmic notes (like a thumb piano, music box, or acoustic harp).

✨ Acoustic Resonance: Every dot rings out with generous decay tails that naturally overlap with following notes like ringing singing bowls!
❙❙❙

Roll / Ratchet

Rapid multi-trigger bursts (like trap hi-hat rolls, drum ruffs, or modular synth ratchets).

✨ Rhythmic Energy: Perfect for adding kinetic movement, rolling shakers, and snappy texture to ambient beats!
Interactive Theremin

✨ Live Play Mode: The Musical Wand

In addition to composing looping scores with ✏️ Draw Mode, Harmonic Geometry includes an expressive ✨ Live Play Mode. Instead of permanently adding lines to your score, clicking and dragging acts like an acoustic musical wand or optical theremin!

✨ Disappearing Ribbon Trail

When you click or drag across the canvas, the note sounds instantly beneath your cursor, leaving behind a glowing, tapering ribbon that smoothly evaporates over 1 second. No permanent strokes clutter your score.

🌌 Customizable Live Reverb & Mix

Live notes default to a standard 50% cathedral reverb, but you can dial it anywhere from 0% dry to 100% ambient wash in ⚙ Controls, alongside a dedicated Live Volume slider to balance against background music.

🎵 Live Scale Lock & Theremin Glide

Switch between Free Glide (continuous microtonal portamento) and Scale Lock (locks onto active scale notes and chord progression tones). In Scale Lock, every touch is guaranteed 100% in tune with your background loops!

🪄 Sustain Tail & Trail Life

Tailor the acoustic release from 0.05s (tight keys) up to 2.5s (soaring ambient pad washes that resonate after lifting your hand). Adjust Trail Life to choose how long the glowing visual ribbon lingers.

💡 Pro Tip: Press P to toggle between ✏️ Draw Score Mode and ✨ Live Play Mode. Open ⚙ Controls at any time—the drawer floats as an overlay without shrinking the canvas or detuning your playing music!

Fine-Tuning

🎛️ The Line Inspector: Sculpting Every Track

Click on any line on the canvas to select it. A glowing Inspector panel appears on the right with powerful controls for that specific line:

🔊 Volume & 🫧 Reverb

Set the individual loudness and reverb room size for each stroke. Give your lead melodies high reverb and your bass clean punch!

⏱️ Speed vs Bars Mode

Choose between Speed (travels at a constant pace) or Bars (locks the travel time to exactly 1, 2, 4, or 8 musical measures).

🎲 Tone Fluctuation (Humanize)

Adds subtle organic micro-variations to the pitch and dot scattering. Set it to 15-30% for that vintage, nostalgic cassette tape warmth.

🌊 Wavify (Sine Wave Restructure)

Want rolling arpeggios without drawing wavy lines by hand? Click Sine Wave in the inspector to turn any straight line into undulating sinusoidal peaks and valleys!

📋 Copy, Paste & Duplicate

Select a line and press ⌘C / ⌘V or ⌘D (or click Duplicate). It clones your curve with all volume, instrument, and speed settings preserved!

🔀 Traversal: Restart vs Bounce

Set how the playhead moves: Restart ↻ (loops from the beginning of your line) or Bounce ⇄ (smoothly oscillates back and forth).

⏱ Timing: Speed vs Timer

Control pacing with Speed (velocity multiplier ¼x to 4x) or Timer (set exact duration from 1s up to 300s). For long meditative drones, simply set the timer high (e.g. 60s, 120s, or 300s).

Pro Advice

💡 10 Golden Tips for Beginners

1. 🏔️ The "Horizon Bass" Trick

Draw one long, straight solid line near the bottom of the screen with Indigo (Cello). This acts as your grounding root tone, giving all other melodies a rich, warm foundation to sit upon.

2. ✨ The "Constellation Melody"

Draw short, gentle curved arches in the top half using Violet (Bells) or Amber (Kalimba) with Dotted (• • •) mode. They will sound like constellations twinkling in the night sky.

3. ⏳ Slow Down the Tempo (24 to 40 BPM)

Pop music runs fast (120 BPM), but ambient music lives in slow time. Set the tempo slider to 28-36 BPM and let the notes ring and breathe.

4. 🎧 Use Stereo Space (Spread Left & Right)

Draw your bass line across the center, one melody on the left half, and another melody on the right half. When wearing headphones, you'll be enveloped in a 3D audio sphere!

5. 📋 The "Duplicate & Harmony" Hack

Draw a melody you love. Select it, press ⌘D to duplicate it, then drag it up by exactly 2 or 4 guideline steps. You just created a perfect two-part harmonic duet!

6. 🥁 The 808 Drum Blueprint

Select Onyx. Draw a dotted line near the bottom for your kick drum rhythm, a dotted line in the middle for your snare clap, and a striped roll line near the top for hi-hat rolls.

7. 📼 Add Fluctuation for Analog Warmth

Computers can sound too perfect. Select your dotted lines and slide Fluctuation to 25%. You'll immediately hear vintage micro-flutter that sounds like an acoustic recording.

8. 🌊 Wavify to Generate Arpeggios

Draw a flat horizontal bar. Select it, and in the inspector set Wave Amp to 40 and Periods to 3. Instant rolling harp arpeggios with zero drawing struggle!

9. Hit "Sync" to Lock, or "Randomize" to Scatter

Press R or click Sync to align all moving playhead dots to line starts simultaneously. Click Randomize (or press Shift+R) to scatter all moving dots to random positions along each line for generative polyrhythms and evolving phase patterns.

10. 📁 Save and Share Your Scores

Created something magical? Open ⚙ Controls and click Save File to download your song as a tiny JSON file. You can load it anytime or share it with friends!
