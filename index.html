Guide

Mathematical Specifications

1. Arc-Length Parameterization Lookup Table (LUT)

Bézier splines parameterized by naive $t \in [0, 1]$ accelerate on flat sections and decelerate in tight bends because the first derivative magnitude $\|B'(t)\|$ varies along the curve.

To achieve uniform velocity traversal at a given musical tempo ($v = \text{BPM} \cdot \frac{80\text{px}}{60\text{s}}$), we construct a monotonic cumulative arc-length table $S[m]$ using composite Simpson's 1/3 quadrature:

Δs ≈ (Δt / 6) · (||B'(t₀)|| + 4||B'(t_mid)|| + ||B'(t₁)||)

Target distance $s^* = (v \cdot \Delta t) \pmod L$ is inverted using binary search, linear parameter interpolation, and a Newton-Raphson refinement step:

t_refined = t* - [s_k(t*) - targetS] / ||B'_k(t*)||

2. Coordinate-to-Pitch Scale Quantizer & Tone Fluctuation

Standard graphics places $(0, 0)$ top-left, while musical frequency increases upwards. Normalized pitch coordinate is inverted:

u = clamp(1 - y / H, 0, 1),   d_cont = u · (N_notes - 1)

Continuous scale degree $d_{\text{cont}}$ smoothly interpolates across modal intervals, with optional per-line tone fluctuation for organic analog drift:

  • Solid Strokes: Glide with smooth, natural microtonal portamento, with tone fluctuation adding organic wandering drift.
  • Dotted / Roll Strokes: Quantized to discrete scale intervals or rapid rhythmic roll/ratchet bursts.

3. Horizontal Axis Spatial Panning & Timbral Color

The continuous horizontal position $x \in [0, W]$ determines stereo spatial placement and high-frequency acoustic sheen:

pan = clamp(2 · (x / W) - 1, -1, 1),   cutoff = baseCutoff · (0.75 + 0.6 · (x / W))

Drawing from left to right physically glides sound through the stereo field from the left channel to the right channel, providing 3D acoustic perspective.
