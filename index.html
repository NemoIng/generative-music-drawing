Algorithm & Mathematical Foundations

1. Arc-Length Parameterization Lookup Table (LUT)

Standard cubic Bézier parameterization $t \in [0, 1]$ causes acceleration along flat spans and deceleration around tight bends due to variable derivative magnitude $\|B'(t)\|$.

We construct a monotonic lookup table $S[m]$ via composite Simpson's 1/3 quadrature across sub-intervals:

Δs ≈ (Δt / 6) · (||B'(t₀)|| + 4||B'(t_mid)|| + ||B'(t₁)||)

Playheads advance at exact physical velocity $v$ (px/s). Distance $s^* = (v \cdot \Delta t) \pmod L$ is inverted via Binary Search + Newton-Raphson refinement:

t_refined = t* - [s_k(t*) - targetS] / ||B'_k(t*)||

2. Coordinate-to-Pitch Scale Quantizer with Deadband Hysteresis

Standard computer graphics places $(0, 0)$ top-left, while musical frequency increases upwards. Normalized pitch coordinate is inverted:

u = clamp(1 - y / H, 0, 1)

Continuous scale degree $d_{\text{cont}} = u \cdot (N_{\text{notes}} - 1)$ is mapped through a center snap deadband $w \in [0, 0.5]$:

  • Solid Strokes: Rest stably on scale degree centers when horizontal, yet glide smoothly with microtonal portamento during vertical moves.
  • Dotted / Striped Strokes: Strictly snapped to discrete modal intervals at precise arc-length pulses.
